The Story Behind FCPC

An Institution Built From One Hard Lesson.

In the early 1980s, Charles Scott entered the life insurance industry at just 22. By his late twenties, he'd broken through as a top producer, tracking toward six figures. Then a severe illness sidelined him for five months.

"With no income coming in, a critical realization hit: if I can't work, who pays the rent? Relying solely on individual production was a fragile foundation for a dream."

That fragility became the founding problem FCPC was built to solve, not just for Charles, but for every advisor who'd ever face the same exposed position. Three principles came out of it, and they've defined FCPC ever since:
