This is an estimate for planning purposes, not a quote or a recommendation. It isn't adjusted for inflation, investment growth, or taxes. Your actual needs can be different.

What we add up

Income your family would need for the years you choose, plus debts, final expenses, an emergency cushion, and education.

What we subtract

Life insurance you already have, through work or on your own, and savings your family could draw on.

Why work coverage matters

Coverage through an employer can reduce or end when you leave or retire. Planning for that ahead of time can be easier than replacing it later.
