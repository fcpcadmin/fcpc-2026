Final Expense Insurance

Guaranteed Life Insurance Coverage

Final expense insurance is built to be simple: guaranteed acceptance for ages 0 to 85, a fixed premium that never increases, and a fixed benefit your family can count on. Coverage begins immediately, so your loved ones are never left carrying the cost alone.

I Want Guaranteed Coverage
Three Powerful Pillars

Simple. Guaranteed. Immediate.

Final expense insurance removes the financial uncertainty of end-of-life planning, delivering guaranteed, immediate coverage with no exam and no surprises.

Guaranteed Acceptance

Ages 0 to 85 qualify, with simplified underwriting and no medical exam required. If you're within the eligible age range, coverage is available to you.

Fixed Premium, Fixed Benefit

Your premium is locked in the day your policy is issued and never increases. Your benefit amount is guaranteed and never decreases. What you sign up for is what your family receives.

Immediate Coverage

Coverage begins the moment your policy is issued, not months later. Depending on your health and eligibility, you may qualify for full coverage right away, or immediate coverage that builds to the full amount over time. Either way, your family is never left waiting.

Who Final Expense Is For

When Traditional Life Insurance Isn't The Right Fit

Whether you're securing coverage for an aging parent or seeking coverage with minimal underwriting for yourself or a loved one due to known health issues, final expense insurance offers a straightforward path to obtaining life insurance. No exam required and minimal health questions.

Final Expense Disclaimer: Final expense insurance is a form of whole life insurance with simplified or guaranteed issue underwriting. Depending on health and eligibility, coverage may be issued at full face value from day one, or as an immediate, partial benefit that increases to the full face amount over a defined period. Eligibility, coverage amounts, premium rates, and qualification criteria vary by carrier and state. Full terms and conditions are disclosed in the policy contract.
