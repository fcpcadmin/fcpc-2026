FEGLI

FEGLI wasn't your choice.
You have other options.

FEGLI comes standard with your federal benefits package. It's opt-out, not opt-in, which means most employees are automatically enrolled from day one. Many also add Option B for extra coverage without ever sitting down with someone to walk through what it actually does, and doesn't do, over the course of a career.

Here's what a lot of families don't find out until it's too late:

  • Your cost keeps climbing.

    Basic FEGLI premiums increase with your salary, while Options A and B increase with your age. Option B in particular can climb sharply the older you get, all while your paycheck may not be scaling to match.

  • Your coverage shrinks right when you need it most.

    By default, FEGLI coverage reduces between 75% and 100% after you retire, right as your other income sources are also shifting.

  • Nothing builds up behind it.

    Every premium dollar is gone the moment you pay it. No cash value, nothing to borrow against, nothing that grows.

  • No living benefits for critical or chronic illness.

    FEGLI does include a Terminal Illness rider, but if you're diagnosed with a chronic illness or suffer a critical illness or injury, it offers nothing while you're alive to need it.

  • It can't become a permanent policy.

    FEGLI doesn't offer the option to convert into permanent coverage, so the protection you've built ends when the group coverage does.

A private policy, chosen with the right guidance, can address many of these gaps. We'll show you, side by side, exactly what you have and exactly what you're missing.

Retirement Income

You've Built Your Retirement Savings.
Are They Actually Protected?

You've spent decades building your retirement savings. The question now isn't solely how to keep it growing but also how to make sure it's kept safe for use in retirement. Whether you plan to use your savings to supplement your pension or social security income, or to let it accumulate safely while accessing it as needed, that's exactly what an indexed annuity is built for:

  • Guaranteed income you can't outlive.

    Turn part of your savings into a paycheck that keeps coming, for as long as you live.

  • Protected principal.

    Your savings are shielded from market downturns — you don't lose ground when the market drops.

  • Market-linked growth potential.

    You still get to participate in market gains, without the downside risk of being directly invested.

  • A safe home for the money you can't afford to gamble with.

    The return of your money is more important than the return on your money.

A study by the American College's Center for Retirement Income found that 62% of recent retirees with substantial assets in an employer-sponsored plan chose to move those assets out of the plan. Employer plans are built for the masses. A personal plan gives you the flexibility to structure your savings around your life, not the other way around.

It's one of the reasons indexed annuities have grown in popularity as a retirement planning tool. If you're 60 or older and want to know exactly how much guaranteed income your savings could generate, we'll walk you through it plainly.
