Indexed Universal Life Insurance

Protection That Adapts as Your Life Does.

Indexed Universal Life insurance combines permanent protection with the flexibility to adjust your coverage and premiums as your needs change, plus the potential to grow cash value tied to market performance — without the risk of market losses.

Talk to a Specialist
Why IUL

Flexibility Built Into Every Part of the Policy.

Life rarely stays the same for 20 or 30 years, and your coverage shouldn't have to either. IUL policies let you adjust your premium payments and death benefit over time as your income, family, and priorities evolve, all within one policy that stays in force for life.

See If IUL Fits Your Situation
Upside Potential, Downside Protection

Your Cash Value Can Grow, But It Won't Go Backward.

An IUL policy's cash value has the potential to grow based on the performance of a market index, offering more upside potential than a traditional whole life policy. But unlike investing directly in the market, your cash value is protected from market downturns. It's a way to participate in growth while keeping a guaranteed floor underneath you.

Explore Cash Value Growth
Living Benefits

Coverage That Can Help You While You're Still Here.

IUL policies can include the option to access a portion of your death benefit while you're still living if you're diagnosed with a qualifying serious illness — a resource that can cover the unexpected without depleting your savings or leaning on your family.

Learn What's Included
Chronic Illness

When Daily Life Becomes a Challenge

Access a portion of your death benefit when a long-term condition limits your ability to perform daily living activities.

Critical Illness

A Diagnosis Can Trigger Early Access

Cancer, heart attack, stroke, and other qualifying diagnoses may allow you to access a portion of your benefit before it's ever needed as a death benefit.

Terminal Illness

Use Your Benefit While You Still Can

A qualifying terminal prognosis may allow you to access your death benefit while you're living — to spend time, settle affairs, or ease the burden on your family.

Lifetime Income

Your Policy Can Help Fund Your Retirement, Too.

Beyond death benefit protection, an IUL policy's cash value can become a source of income later in life, supplementing retirement savings alongside your other accounts. Some policies can even offer the potential for guaranteed income you can't outlive.
