Living Benefits — Built In

Your IUL Works for You
Before You're Gone.

Modern IUL contracts from leading carriers include Living Benefits riders — provisions that allow you to accelerate a portion of your death benefit during your lifetime if you experience a qualifying chronic, critical, or terminal illness event.

This means the wealth your IUL accumulates is not locked away until death. A serious diagnosis does not have to mean liquidating retirement accounts, selling property, or burdening your children. Your policy activates as a financial shield — protecting the generational wealth you've built and giving your family stability exactly when the stakes are highest.

Chronic Illness

Long-Term Care & Assistance

If a chronic illness prevents you from performing activities of daily living, your IUL's accelerated benefit can help fund in-home care, assisted living, or memory care — without draining your retirement savings or the inheritance you've built for your children.

Critical Illness

Cancer, Heart Attack & Stroke

A critical diagnosis generates costs that health insurance rarely covers fully — lost income, travel, alternative treatments, and family support. A critical illness acceleration on your IUL provides a lump sum to address these gaps without touching your core wealth strategy.

Terminal Illness

Dignity, Legacy & Final Wishes

A terminal diagnosis with a qualifying life expectancy can trigger an early advance of your benefit — giving you the resources to fulfill final wishes, settle your estate, and ensure your family is protected and provided for before you are gone.
