Final Expense Disclaimer: Final expense insurance is a form of whole life insurance with simplified or guaranteed issue underwriting. Depending on health and eligibility, coverage may be issued at full face value from day one, or as an immediate, partial benefit that increases to the full face amount over a defined period. Eligibility, coverage amounts, premium rates, and qualification criteria vary by carrier and state. Full terms and conditions are disclosed in the policy contract.
