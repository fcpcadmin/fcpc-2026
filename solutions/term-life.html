Why Term Life

Protecting What's Temporary, For As Long As It's Temporary.

A mortgage, years of raising children, replacing income during your highest-earning working years. These are real financial responsibilities with a natural endpoint.

Term life insurance is designed around that. You choose the coverage amount and the length of time, and your family is protected for exactly the years it matters most, without paying for a lifetime of coverage you may not need.

See If Term Life Fits Your Situation
Built-In Flexibility

More Than Just a Death Benefit.

It's common to see families choose a more substantial term policy, not just to cover today's needs, but because of what's built into the policy itself.

Many term policies include the option to access a portion of your benefit early if you're diagnosed with a qualifying serious illness. And most include the ability to convert to permanent coverage later without requalifying medically, which means locking in your insurability now, while you're healthy, even if your needs change down the road.

Living Benefits

Access Your Benefit While You're Alive

Many term policies include riders that allow you to access a portion of your death benefit if you're diagnosed with a qualifying chronic, critical, or terminal illness — so your policy isn't only triggered by death.

Conversion Option

Lock In Your Insurability Now

Most term policies include the right to convert to permanent coverage without a new medical exam. Your health status today locks in your insurability for the future, even if it changes.
