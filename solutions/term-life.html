Conversion Privileges

Flexibility for the Future.
Guaranteed Convertibility.

Most Term Life policies include a guaranteed conversion provision — a contractual right that lets you convert your temporary coverage into a permanent policy at any time during the eligible conversion period, without submitting to a new medical exam or answering additional health questions.

This means that even if your health changes after your policy is issued — a new diagnosis, a chronic condition, a decline in insurability — your right to convert is fully protected. You cannot be denied. You simply exercise the provision and your Term policy becomes a permanent Indexed Universal Life (IUL) or Whole Life plan, carrying forward all of your original health classification and rate tier.

Conversion is one of the most underutilized benefits in insurance. It transforms a simple, affordable term policy into a long-term strategic asset — giving your family the protection they need today and the permanence they may need tomorrow.

Living Benefits — Built In

Protection That Works
While You're Still Here.

Many Term Life policies include Living Benefits riders that let you accelerate a portion of your death benefit during your lifetime if you face a qualifying chronic, critical, or terminal illness diagnosis.

A serious diagnosis shouldn't mean draining your savings or selling assets to cover treatment. Your Term Life policy can step in — giving your family financial breathing room at the exact moment it's needed most, without waiting until death to provide relief.

Chronic Illness

Long-Term Care Costs

If a chronic illness prevents two or more daily living activities, your policy may allow you to accelerate benefits to cover in-home care, assisted living, or nursing facilities — preserving your savings and your family's financial foundation.

Critical Illness

Heart Attack, Stroke & Cancer

A critical illness event can be financially devastating. A lump-sum advance from your death benefit covers treatment gaps, lost income, and household expenses that health insurance leaves behind — without depleting your retirement savings.

Terminal Illness

Dignity & Final Wishes

If a terminal diagnosis gives you a limited prognosis, you may qualify to access a portion of your benefit early — allowing you to fulfill final wishes, reduce your family's burden, and ensure your affairs are in order before you are gone.
