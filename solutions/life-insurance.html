Living Benefits

Your Policy Can Protect You
While You're Still Living.

Modern life insurance is no longer just a death benefit. Many of today's policies include Living Benefits riders — provisions that allow you to access a portion of your own death benefit while you are still alive, triggered by a qualifying chronic, critical, or terminal illness diagnosis.

This means a serious illness doesn't have to drain your savings, liquidate your retirement accounts, or burden your children. Your life insurance policy becomes a financial shield at exactly the moment your family needs it most — protecting both your immediate stability and the generational wealth you've spent a lifetime building.

Chronic Illness

Long-Term Care Costs

If you are unable to perform two or more activities of daily living, a chronic illness rider can accelerate your benefit — helping cover in-home care, assisted living, or nursing facility costs without depleting your estate.

Critical Illness

Heart Attack, Stroke & Cancer

A heart attack, stroke, or cancer diagnosis can generate catastrophic out-of-pocket costs even with health insurance. A critical illness rider provides a lump-sum advance to cover treatment, lost income, and family expenses.

Terminal Illness

Dignity & Final Wishes

A terminal diagnosis with a life expectancy of 12–24 months may allow you to access your benefit early — so you can fulfill final wishes, settle affairs, and ensure your family is financially secure before you're gone.
