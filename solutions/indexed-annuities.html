Lifetime Income 01

Guaranteed Lifetime Income

Designed for individuals who want the certainty of a reliable, predictable stream of income in retirement — a paycheck-style monthly deposit that continues for as long as you live, no matter how long that turns out to be.

Through an income rider added to your indexed annuity contract, you lock in a guaranteed withdrawal rate on a growing income base — separate from your account value — that you can activate when you are ready to retire. The income benefit continues for life, regardless of whether your account value is eventually depleted, completely eliminating the risk of outliving your savings.

This pathway is right for you if:

You are approaching or in retirement and want income you cannot outlive

You want to replace or supplement a pension or Social Security check

Sequence-of-returns risk keeps you up at night — you need income certainty

Capital Preservation 02

Long-Term Capital Preservation

Designed for individuals who have accumulated significant savings and need a completely safe, contractually protected home for that wealth — one that grows with the market in good years and never gives back a dollar in bad years.

Your principal is fully protected by the insurance carrier's 0% floor guarantee. In years when the linked index rises, a portion of those gains is credited to your account and permanently locked in — they cannot be taken back by future market downturns. In years when the index falls, your account simply holds at its current value. The result is a steadily ratcheting balance that only moves in one direction: up.

This pathway is right for you if:

You have retirement savings in CDs, bonds, or money market accounts earning minimal interest

You cannot afford to lose what you have — protection of principal is non-negotiable

You want market participation without market risk — upside without sleepless nights

The Most Important Concept in Retirement Planning

The 0% Floor.
Your Gains Lock In. Your Losses Don't.

The 0% floor is not a marketing phrase — it is a contractual guarantee written into the insurance policy itself. It means that in any given contract year when the linked market index declines, your account value does not decline. The floor absorbs the loss. Your balance holds.

In positive market years, your annuity is credited with a portion of the index's gain — subject to the carrier's participation rate, cap, or spread. Once that gain is credited to your account, it is permanently locked in. Future down markets cannot reclaim it. This ratchet mechanism — gains stick, losses don't — is what makes indexed annuities uniquely powerful in a retirement portfolio.

The compound effect over a 10 or 20 year accumulation period is significant. A traditional investment account that loses 30% in a bad year needs a 43% gain just to recover to even. An indexed annuity simply holds at its prior high-water mark and begins growing again the following year from that protected base.
