Indexed Annuities

Protect Your Hard-Earned Retirement Savings, With Room to Grow.

An indexed annuity lets your savings grow alongside the market in good years, while contractually protecting your principal from ever losing value in bad ones. You get to participate in the upside, fully shielded from the downside.

Two Powerful Pathways

Guaranteed Security. Two Ways to Get There.

Whether you need a guaranteed paycheck for life or a safe place to grow your savings without market risk, there's an indexed annuity strategy built for your situation.

Lifetime Income 01

Guaranteed Lifetime Income

For those who want the certainty of a reliable income they can never outlive. An income rider added to your annuity can turn your savings into a paycheck that continues for as long as you live, no matter how long that turns out to be.

This pathway fits you if:

You're approaching or in retirement and want income you can't outlive

You want to replace or supplement a pension or Social Security check

The idea of running out of money in retirement keeps you up at night

Capital Preservation 02

Long-Term Capital Preservation

For those who've built savings and need a genuinely safe place to keep it — one that can still grow with the market in good years, without ever losing ground in bad ones.

This pathway fits you if:

You have savings sitting in CDs, bonds, or money market accounts earning minimal interest

Protecting what you've already built matters more to you than chasing higher returns

You want market participation without market risk

The Core Concept

Your Gains Lock In.
Your Losses Don't.

The 0% floor is a contractual guarantee: in any year the market index declines, your account value doesn't decline with it. When the market rises, a portion of that gain gets credited to your account and locked in for good. It's growth that only moves in one direction.
