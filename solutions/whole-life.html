Living Benefits — Built In

Your Policy Protects You
Before You're Gone.

Many modern Whole Life contracts include Living Benefits riders that allow you to accelerate a portion of your death benefit during your lifetime if you face a qualifying chronic, critical, or terminal illness.

This means your Whole Life policy isn't only activated at death. A serious diagnosis doesn't have to drain your retirement savings or burden your children. Your policy becomes an active shield — protecting the generational wealth you've built and giving your family financial stability at the moment of highest need.

Chronic Illness

Long-Term Care Costs

If a chronic illness prevents daily living activities, your policy can accelerate benefits to cover in-home care, assisted living, or nursing facilities — preserving your retirement savings and the inheritance you're building.

Critical Illness

Heart Attack, Stroke & Cancer

A critical illness rider provides a lump-sum advance to cover treatment gaps, lost income, and family expenses that health insurance doesn't fully address — without touching your core wealth strategy.

Terminal Illness

Dignity & Final Wishes

A terminal diagnosis may allow early access to your benefit — giving you resources to fulfill final wishes, settle your estate, and ensure your family is protected and provided for before you are gone.
