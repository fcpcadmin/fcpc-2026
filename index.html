You Never Really "Chose" FEGLI.
Here's What It's Not Telling You.

FEGLI comes standard with your federal benefits package. It's opt-out, not opt-in, which means most employees are automatically enrolled from day one. Many also add Option B for extra coverage without ever sitting down with someone to walk through what it actually does, and doesn't do, over the course of a career.

Here's what a lot of families don't find out until it's too late:

  • Your cost keeps climbing.

    Basic FEGLI premiums increase with your salary, while Options A and B increase with your age. Option B in particular can climb sharply the older you get, all while your paycheck isn't scaling to match.

  • Your coverage shrinks right when you need it most.

    By default, FEGLI coverage reduces between 75% and 100% after you retire, right as your other income sources are also shifting.

  • Nothing builds up behind it.

    Every premium dollar is gone the moment you pay it — no cash value, nothing to borrow against, nothing that grows.

  • No living benefits for critical or chronic illness.

    FEGLI does include a Terminal Illness rider, but if you're diagnosed with a chronic illness or suffer a critical illness or injury, it offers nothing while you're alive to need it.

  • It can't become a permanent policy.

    FEGLI doesn't offer the option to convert into permanent coverage, so the protection you've built ends when the group coverage does.

A private policy, chosen with the right guidance, can solve every one of these gaps. We'll show you, side by side, exactly what you have and exactly what you're missing. Then it's your call.

Retirement Income

Your Savings Worked Hard.
Now Make Sure They Can't Disappear.

You've spent decades building your retirement savings. The question now isn't solely how to keep it growing — it's how to make sure to keep it safe and how best to use it in retirement. Whether you plan to use your savings to supplement your pension or social security income, or to let it accumulate safely while accessing it as needed, that's exactly what an indexed annuity is built for:

  • Guaranteed income you can't outlive.

    Turn part of your savings into a paycheck that keeps coming, for as long as you live.

  • Protected principal.

    Your savings are shielded from market downturns — you don't lose ground when the market drops.

  • Market-linked growth potential.

    You still get to participate in market gains, without the downside risk of being directly invested.

  • A safe home for the money you can't afford to gamble with.

    This isn't about chasing returns — it's about knowing it will be there.

62% of retirees move their retirement savings out of their employer-sponsored plan as soon as they're eligible. Employer plans are built for the masses — a personal plan gives you the flexibility to structure your savings around your life, not the other way around.

It's why indexed annuities are one of the fastest-growing areas of retirement planning in the country right now. If you're 60 or older and want to know exactly how much guaranteed income your savings could generate, we'll walk you through it plainly.
